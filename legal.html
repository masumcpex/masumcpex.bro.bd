Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Attendance app ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you sign in and use the app to track attendance and working hours for your team.

Information we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with email/password, Google, or Facebook, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo (if provided by that sign-in method).
  • Attendance data: daily attendance records, status (present/absent), and timestamps that you or your team enter into the app.
  • Team member data: names of team members added to the app, and who added them.

How we use this information

  • To let you sign in securely and identify your account.
  • To display your name and profile photo inside the app (e.g. in the account menu).
  • To record and display attendance and working-hour data for your team.
  • To generate summaries and downloadable reports (e.g. PDF) at your request.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

How we store and protect information

Data is stored using Firebase (a Google Cloud service) with access rules that require you to be signed in before any data can be read or written. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

Sign in with Google and Facebook

If you choose to sign in with Google or Facebook, we only request basic profile information (name, email address, and profile photo) needed to create and identify your account. We do not post to your Google or Facebook account or access any other data from those services.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and attendance data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the app's functionality. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us using the details below.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for workplace attendance tracking and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
